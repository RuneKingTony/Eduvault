- Status: draft (pre-spec)
- Source: brand prototype `https://claude.ai/artifact/1hsDyr1KdUaYD2hnuA9sfY`, version `1791539787-0c53` (CSS in the prototype's `<style>` block; UI helpers in script lines 140–470; shells in 467–880)

## Summary

Every screen in the prototype is built from about forty shared patterns on one brand palette (deep teal, teal, mint, gold). This spec moves that palette into `libs/ui/src/styles/theme.css` as shadcn variables, lists each pattern as an existing `libs/ui` component, a shadcn component to add, or an in-house component to build, and specifies the visual pieces the two app shells are built from (staff `web-admin` with a sidebar, students and guardians `web-portal` with a top bar and phone bottom nav). What the shells show and when is specified in [10](10-app-shell-and-navigation.md). It also fixes the formatting helpers every screen relies on: naira from minor units, dates, initials avatars. After this slice a module PRD can say "a `StatCard`" or "a `MoneyTile`" and mean one thing.

### Brand layer (motion and surfaces)

- Cards: a 1px ring plus a three-step soft shadow tinted with `--brand-deep`; clickable cards lift 1px on hover with a teal ring.
- Primary button: `--primary`, hover `--primary-hover`, and a light sweep across the button on hover (700ms, `--ease-out`).
- Focus: buttons and links show a 2px background gap then a 2px `--brand-teal` ring at 60%; inputs show a teal border and a 3px teal halo at 18%.
- Sidebar and portal top bar carry a 1px gold gradient rule under the header.
- Page reveal: each top-level block of `main` fades in and slides 12px over 350ms with a 35ms stagger, capped at 12 children, only on route change.
- Skeleton: a shimmer gradient over `--muted`, 1.6s linear.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` turns every animation and transition off.
- The load splash ("Opening the vault", script line 1045 of the HTML) is not ported: a 3s splash delays every cold load. The page reveal above is (DS-08).

## Business rules

1. Every colour in `apps/` and `libs/ui` comes from a theme token through a Tailwind utility (`bg-primary`, `text-muted-foreground`, `bg-destructive/10`). No hex, `rgb()` or palette class (`bg-blue-600`) outside `theme.css`.
2. `theme.css` holds the prototype's light and dark values for every token in the tables above, and registers each new token under `@theme inline` so Tailwind generates its utilities.
3. Components come only from `pnpm ui:add <name>` followed by `pnpm ui:localize`. `components/ui/` stays CLI output; behaviour changes go into a `components/custom/` wrapper whose name is not a shadcn name. New components are exported from `libs/ui/src/index.ts` in the right block.
4. `libs/ui` stays presentational: permission checks (`Can`, `useCan`) live in `libs/auth-client`; shells live in each app's `src/components/`, assembled from `libs/ui` pieces.
5. Dark mode follows the person's system setting until they choose "Light or dark", then their choice persists on that device. The mechanism is shadcn's `.dark` class (`@custom-variant dark`) set by a small theme hook that reads `prefers-color-scheme` until the person picks, and stores the choice in `localStorage` ([D-046](../technical-reference.md#decision-log)).
6. The school brand colour (prototype setting "Look and wording", currently hidden) overrides only `--primary`, `--primary-hover` and `--primary-light` in light mode, from a fixed list (Teal `#115E59`, Blue `#1D4ED8`, Purple `#6D28D9`, Maroon `#9F1239`). It never changes dark mode. Out of scope for M0.1; the token layer must allow it.
7. `naira(minor, opts)` formats a whole number of minor units: divides by 100, groups with `en-NG` separators, shows no decimals unless the value has kobo (up to 2) or `opts.kobo` forces 2. Negative values show a U+2212 minus before the sign (`−₦5,000`), or parentheses with `opts.paren` (`(₦5,000)`); `opts.plus` prefixes `+` on positive values. Zero shows `₦0`. `naira`, naira parsing, `fmtDate` and `initials` live in `libs/shared`, because the API needs them for receipt and statement text; the avatar hue lives in `libs/ui` ([D-046](../technical-reference.md#decision-log)).
8. A naira input accepts digits with optional thousands commas and up to two decimals, returns minor units as an integer, and refuses a minus sign, letters and a third decimal with a field error. It never parses through a float in a way that loses a kobo (parse the string's integer and fraction parts separately).
9. `fmtDate(isoDate)` formats a calendar date (a `DATE` column such as `paid_on`, `starts_on`) as `D Mon YYYY` (`7 Oct 2026`) from the `YYYY-MM-DD` part with no timezone conversion; null shows "—". Ranges show "12 Oct 2026 – 13 Oct 2026", or one date when both ends match. A timestamp (`posted_at`, audit time) is first converted to Africa/Lagos through the one time helper, then formatted the same way. There is no per-school time zone ([D-023](../technical-reference.md#decision-log)).
10. `initials(name)` takes the first letter of the first two words, upper-cased ("Funmi Adeyemi" → "FA", "Ada" → "A").
11. The avatar hue is a deterministic function of the name (`h = (h × 31 + charCode) mod 360` over its characters), so one person has one colour on every screen and in both apps. The hue is quantised to 24 buckets of 15°, each a generated class (`avatar-h-0` … `avatar-h-23`) in `theme.css`, so no `style` prop is needed (DS-03).
12. Every icon-only control has an accessible name, and its tooltip shows that name.
13. Tables page at 10 rows; a list of 10 or fewer has no pager.
14. Payment proof (`ProofInput` on Record a payment) accepts JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC and PDF. It is required when the method is bank transfer and hidden otherwise. Copy: label "Proof of transfer", marker "(needed for bank transfers)", hint "A photo, a screenshot from WhatsApp or the bank app, or a PDF. It is kept with the receipt."; missing: "Add the proof of transfer (a photo, screenshot or PDF) before recording."; wrong type: "The proof must be a photo, a screenshot or a PDF."
15. Store payment receipt (`ProofInput` on Record student purchase) is required for POS and transfer and hidden for cash. Copy: label "Payment receipt", marker "(required for POS and transfer)", hint "Upload the POS slip or the bank transfer receipt (photo or PDF)."; missing: "Upload the POS slip before recording." or "Upload the transfer receipt before recording."
16. Logo and guardian photo accept PNG, JPG and WebP up to 1 MB. Errors: "Choose a PNG, JPG or WebP logo." / "That logo is over 1 MB. Please choose a smaller one."; "Choose a PNG, JPG or WebP photo." / "That photo is over 1 MB. Please choose a smaller one."

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given `theme.css` after this slice, when a reviewer compares it with the prototype's `:root` and dark blocks, then every token in the tables above has the prototype's value in both modes, and `--chrome-*` and the alias tokens are absent.
2. Given any `.tsx` file in `apps/` or `libs/ui`, when searched for `#[0-9a-fA-F]{3,8}`, `rgb(` or a Tailwind palette class, then nothing is found outside `theme.css`.
3. Given a page `h1`, when rendered, then it uses Playfair Display 600; body text uses Inter; a receipt number uses Roboto Mono; no font is requested from a third-party host.
4. Given `naira(1234567)`, then it returns `₦12,345.67`; `naira(1800000000)` returns `₦18,000,000`; `naira(-500000)` returns `−₦5,000`; `naira(-500000, { paren: true })` returns `(₦5,000)`; `naira(0)` returns `₦0`.
5. Given a naira input with "15,000", when the form submits, then the value is `1500000`; "15000.505" shows a field error; "-200" shows a field error.
6. Given `fmtDate('2026-10-07')` in a browser set to UTC−5, then it shows `7 Oct 2026` (no shift); given the timestamp `2026-10-07T23:30:00Z`, then the timestamp formatter shows `8 Oct 2026` (Lagos time).
7. Given the same name on two screens, then both avatars have the same hue and initials.
8. Given a table with 23 rows, when it renders, then it shows rows 1–10, "Showing 1 to 10 of 23", pages 1, 2, 3, and "Previous" disabled; given 10 rows, no pager shows.
9. Given a viewport of 600px, when a table renders, then each row is a stacked card whose labels are the column headers, and a dialog opens as a bottom sheet.
10. Given the `Sidebar` collapsed to a rail, when it renders, then it is 3.25rem wide, labels, counts and group labels are hidden, and each link shows a tooltip; given `CommandDialog` opened, then focus is in its input and Escape closes it.
11. Given a person whose system is dark, when they first open either app, then it renders dark; when they choose "Light or dark", then it switches and stays switched after reload.
12. Given `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, then no reveal, sweep, shimmer or overlay animation runs.
13. Given `ChildChips` with three children and `PortalBottomNav` with four items on a 390px screen, when they render, then the chips scroll sideways with `aria-pressed` on the selected one, and the bottom nav is fixed with a 20px icon and a short label per item.
14. Given a bank transfer on Record a payment with no proof, when submitted, then the field shows "Add the proof of transfer (a photo, screenshot or PDF) before recording." and nothing is sent.
15. Given a 2 MB logo, when chosen, then "That logo is over 1 MB. Please choose a smaller one." shows and the input clears.
16. Given a balance of −₦5,000, when `BalancePill` renders, then it shows "₦5,000 extra paid" in the credit colour; ₦0 shows "Paid up"; ₦12,000 shows "₦12,000" in the owe colour.
17. Given every component in the catalogue, when rendered in light and dark, then text on its background meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large and icons).

## Prototype gaps noticed

- **G-01** Light-mode `--warning-foreground` is `#FFFFFF`, but `warning` badges, `warn` icon squares and toasts draw that colour as text on a 22–25% gold tint, which is white on pale gold (unreadable). Use a dark ink for text on warning tints (for example `#5C4510` light) or keep the shadcn badge's `text-warning-foreground` only on solid `bg-warning`.
- **G-02** The "extra paid" blue `#2563EB` is a literal in five rules with no dark value and no token.
- **G-03** `--sidebar-ring` is not set; the brand layer paints the sidebar with `--brand-deep` and ignores `--sidebar`.
- **G-04** The payment proof accepts HEIC but the store receipt accepts `image/*`; neither has a size limit. Logo and photo cap at 1 MB. Pick one rule per kind (see [03](03-foundation-ledger-and-documents.md#file-storage-for-uploads)).
- **G-05** Proof is never viewable: the payment shows only the file name with a `file-check` icon, and a store sale's "Paid · RCT-…" badge only has a tooltip "Receipt uploaded: <name>". The product needs a view/download action gated like the payment itself.
- **G-06** The naira parser (`finMinor`) strips every character except digits and dots, so "-500" becomes ₦500 and "1.2.3" becomes ₦1.20. Rule 8 replaces it.
- **G-07** The prototype's "Look and wording" settings page (brand colour, receipt footer, sign-off) is defined but hidden from the settings nav; the date-and-number-format rows say "not active".
- **G-08** Fonts come from Google Fonts in the prototype; the apps self-host.
- **G-09** Heading font differs between the prototype (Inter for h2–h4, Playfair for h1) and today's `libs/ui` (Roboto for h1–h3).
